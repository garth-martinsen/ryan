Timeline for AdcClient processing:
Assuming volts_period = 3600 sec, amps_period = 60 sec, 

Seconds: 0........60.......120......3600... repeats.
         |1.
         |2.      |2.        |2.  ...   |3.

Legend:
1- amps_adc saves start_timestamp for start of measurement period, 
2.-amps_adc (every 60 seconds), Measures amps, increment counter,computes (convert mean a2d to volts), i=Vs/Rs, accumulate amps...
3.- End of Measurement period. 
    - AdcClient generates next meas_id = meas_id +1, code=code+1, copy of incoming MSGID, and fills in preliminary required fields of report_msg.
    - AdcClient sends report_msg to amps_adc and volts_adc so they can fill in their fields of the report.
    - amps_adc saves end_timestamp, computes voltage period in seconds, the I_MEAN=amps_accum/count, and ah_used. 
    - volts_adc adds a2d arrays for each channel and adds other required fields. 
    - AdcClient sends completed json-worthy msg to SVR.
4. - Repeat of above for as long as it is acting.

sts = start_timestamp
ets = end_timestamp
amps_adc = Analog digital converter for measuring total current. Uses Rs = 50mΩ and differential opAmp with gain of 20. 
volts_adc = Analog digital converter for measuring Voltages at Voltage Taps(OneCell, TwoCells, ThreeCells).
report_msg = Factory-generated msg form used by AdcClient, when it is fully populated, to send report to Svr.
The report msg follows, values will be set/changed by: AdcClient, AmpsAdc, VoltsAdc : 

ADC_TO_SVR_TEMPLATE = { "SENDER":"ADC", "RECEIVER":"SVR", "APP_ID":1, "VERSION":3, "CODE": 101, "MSGID": 123, "MEAS_ID":1, "TYPE":"m", "CHANS":[ 
   {"CHAN":0, "TIMESTAMP": 178000.0, "VIN":0 , "SAMP_SZ": 64, "A2D":[24500,24500]},      #A2D will have 64 values.. just dummy here...
